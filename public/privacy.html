Privacy Policy

Qraft is designed to create QR codes with as little data collection as possible. This policy explains what happens when you use the website.

QR code content and uploaded logos

Your QR code content, design settings and uploaded logo are processed locally in your browser. Qraft does not require an account and does not intentionally upload this content to our servers. Downloads are generated on your device.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information—such as IP address, browser type, device type, request time and requested page—to deliver the website, prevent abuse and maintain security.

Advertising and Google AdSense

If and when advertising is enabled, Qraft may use Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses and similar identifiers, to serve, measure and personalize ads where permitted. Learn more about how Google uses data on partner sites and manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings.

Cookies and consent

Essential storage may be used to keep website preferences. Where legally required, we will request consent before using non-essential advertising or analytics cookies. You may also control cookies through your browser settings.

External links

QR destinations and external links are controlled by their respective website owners. Qraft is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Children's privacy

Qraft is a general-purpose design utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@antss.in.
