ABOUT THE PROJECT

QR codes should be useful—and look good.

Qraft is a free custom QR code generator built to make branded, reliable QR codes accessible without requiring an account or sending design data to a server.

Why Qraft exists

Many QR tools hide basic customization behind sign-ups, subscriptions or confusing dashboards. Qraft focuses on the useful core: add a destination, choose a visual style, test the result and download it.

Designed for real projects

The generator includes editable colors, dot patterns, logo support, high error correction and ready-made themes for menus, events, packaging, business cards, posters and social profiles.

Privacy by design

Generation happens in your browser. The text, URL and logo used in your QR code remain on your device during the creation process.

Our approach

We care about honest claims, accessible design, scan reliability and a calm interface. Qraft will continue to improve around those principles.
